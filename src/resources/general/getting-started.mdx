# Getting Started with AI

If you have never programmed before, **start with Python**. You do not need to learn all of computer science before touching AI.

## Your first path

1. **Python** — variables, loops, functions, lists, dictionaries, files, and debugging.
2. **Data & tools** — NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, and notebooks.
3. **Basic math** — probability, statistics, vectors, matrices, derivatives, and optimization intuition.
4. **Machine learning** — learn how models learn from data and how to evaluate them.
5. **Deep learning** — neural networks and PyTorch.
6. **AI domains** — computer vision, NLP, and audio.

The [IOAI Learning Roadmap](../../roadmap) gives you the full recommended order.

## What to focus on

- **Understand, don't just memorize.** Know what your code is doing.
- **Code early.** Turn each idea into a small experiment.
- **Use real data.** Small datasets are enough to build intuition.
- **Practice before you feel ready.** You can start simple problems while learning the theory.

> You do not need to know everything before your first AI project. You need a clear next step.
