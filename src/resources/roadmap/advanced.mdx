# Advanced AI

**Level:** 🔴 Advanced
**Prerequisites:** Strong ML, deep learning and at least one AI domain

This stage is for deeper study after the foundation is strong.

## Explore

- Transformers and modern architectures
- Generative models
- Multimodal AI
- Reinforcement learning
- Efficient training and inference
- Reading and reproducing research

## ⭐ Main resource

**Hugging Face Learn**  
https://huggingface.co/learn

Choose the course or topic that matches your direction instead of trying to complete everything.

## Research

**Papers With Code**  
https://paperswithcode.com/

Use it to find papers, implementations and benchmarks when you want to reproduce a method.

## Move on when...

You can read a modern AI paper, understand its main idea, and implement or adapt a non-trivial method at a small scale.
