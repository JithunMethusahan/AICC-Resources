# Mathematics for AI

**Level:** 🟢 Beginner → 🟡 Intermediate

Build the math foundation alongside your AI learning. You do not need to complete a full university mathematics degree first.

## Learn in this order

1. Algebra and functions
2. Probability
3. Statistics
4. Vectors and matrices
5. Derivatives and gradients
6. Optimization and gradient descent

## Move on when...

You can work with vectors and matrices, understand basic probability and statistics, and explain a gradient and optimization in simple words.
