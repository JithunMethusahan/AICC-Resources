# AI Domains

**Level:** 🟡 Intermediate → 🔴 Advanced
**Prerequisites:** ML + deep learning basics

After the core foundation, explore domains. You do not need to master every domain.

## Computer Vision

**Stanford CS231n**  
https://cs231n.stanford.edu/

Learn image classification, CNNs, detection and modern visual models.

## Natural Language Processing

**Stanford CS224N**  
https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224n/

Learn language representations, neural NLP and transformers.

## Modern AI

**Hugging Face Course**  
https://huggingface.co/learn

A practical route into transformers, datasets and modern NLP/AI tooling.

## Audio

**Hugging Face Audio Course**  
https://huggingface.co/learn/audio-course

Use this if you want to explore speech and audio models.

## Move on when...

You can choose one domain, understand its data representation, and build a small end-to-end model or experiment.
