# Language Models

A language model learns patterns in text and can assign probabilities to or generate sequences of tokens. Modern systems are usually based on transformer architectures, but older approaches are still useful for understanding the foundations and for some constrained tasks.

## Types

- **Autoregressive models:** predict the next token.
- **Masked language models:** predict hidden tokens from surrounding context.
- **Encoder-decoder models:** map one sequence to another, such as translation.

## What to learn

Understand tokenization, embeddings, attention, transformer blocks, training objectives, and evaluation. Then learn how to adapt pretrained models to classification, sequence labeling, or generation tasks.

## Competition perspective

Fine-tuning a pretrained model can be powerful, but it is not automatically the right answer. Compare it with simpler approaches and consider dataset size, compute, time, memory, and the evaluation metric.
